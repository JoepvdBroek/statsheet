# Adopt free-exercise-db's 17 Muscles, with no Muscle Groups

The Exercise catalogue is a one-time import of free-exercise-db's strength-type exercises (strength, powerlifting, olympic weightlifting, strongman; text only, since its images have unclear provenance). After import it is the owner's own data, freely edited and extended, with no upstream sync. We adopt its flat list of 17 Muscles unchanged (abdominals, abductors, adductors, biceps, calves, chest, forearms, glutes, hamstrings, lats, lower back, middle back, neck, quadriceps, shoulders, traps, triceps) as the only level that volume and goals work on. We chose it over wger (CC-BY-SA with per-exercise attribution, anatomical muscles with gaps, many exercises without muscles) and over our own mapped list (every seeded exercise would need remapping). We deliberately have no Muscle Groups such as "Arms" or "Legs": summing opposing Muscles (biceps with triceps, quads with calves) gives volume nobody can act on, and group goals would double count sets. The accepted cost: there is no front/side/rear deltoid split, because "shoulders" is one Muscle.
